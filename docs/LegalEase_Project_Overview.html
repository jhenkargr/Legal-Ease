LegalEase System Overview

AI-Powered Legal Document Simplification & Autonomous Legal Advisory Platform

Dual-Engine Microservices Production Verified
Full-Stack RAG & OCR
Executive Summary: Legal agreements, NDAs, and tenancy contracts are notoriously opaque, creating severe asymmetric risk for citizens and small businesses. LegalEase bridges this literacy gap by transforming convoluted legal contracts into actionable, plain-language summaries with highlighted risk alerts. The platform couples client-side hybrid PDF/OCR ingestion with a dual AI inference pipeline (Cohere v2 Command-R + NVIDIA NIM Fallback) and a vector-grounded RAG service (FAISS + Google Gemini) to deliver authoritative legal guidance in under 4 seconds.

End-to-End System Architecture
Key Engineering Contributions & Innovations
⚡ Dual-AI Engine & JSON Strictness
Eliminated brittle markdown parsing by architecting a prioritized Cohere v2 Chat pipeline with strict json_object response formatting, backed by NVIDIA NIM as hot fallback. Reduced latency from 50s to <4s with 100% schema compliance.
📄 Low-Latency Hybrid PDF Ingestion
Replaced client-side 45MB multilingual OCR downloads with an instant digital text stream extractor using PDF.js (<100ms). Embedded intelligent fallback to English Tesseract OCR for scanned physical deeds without browser lockups.
🛡️ Resilient DB Pooling & RAG Context
Resolved MySQL idle disconnect crashes (PROTOCOL_CONNECTION_LOST) by migrating to connection pooling. Restructured FastAPI vector memory to dynamically sync 117 embedded legal chunks with FAISS for reliable legal queries.

Key Functional Workflows
1. Document Simplifier & Risk Analyzer Core Module
Users upload contracts (PDF, DOCX, Image). The hybrid engine extracts text in milliseconds, streams it to the Cohere v2 JSON engine, and produces an executive summary alongside categorized key clauses with red warning badges on high-risk covenants (liquidated damages, non-competes).
2. "Know Your Rights" Conversational RAG FAISS Grounded
Citizens can query legal rights via text or voice. Queries are converted to 384-dimensional dense vectors, retrieved from the FAISS knowledge store (Model Tenancy Act & Constitutional rights), and synthesized into authoritative, grounded advice via Google Gemini.
3. Verified Lawyer Directory MySQL Powered
Connects individuals with licensed advocates across jurisdictions. Supports search filtering by legal domain (Family, Corporate, Criminal, Real Estate), geographical jurisdiction, pricing, bar admission, and client ratings with instant booking triggers.
4. Multilingual Legal Translation & Scaffolding Inclusive Access
Enables instantaneous translation of analyzed documents and rights advice into regional Indian languages (Hindi, Kannada) while retaining structured legal clause status and risk indicators. Provides battle-tested contract templates for immediate download.
